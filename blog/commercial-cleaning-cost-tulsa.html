How Much Does Commercial Cleaning Cost in Tulsa?

One of the first questions we hear from Tulsa businesses is: "What's this going to cost?" It's a fair question. Commercial cleaning is a real operating expense, and you deserve transparency about pricing.

The short answer: commercial cleaning costs vary widely based on your facility's size, condition, service frequency, and what you need cleaned. But let's break down the factors that drive cost so you can understand what you're paying for.

What Affects Commercial Cleaning Cost?

Facility Size

Larger facilities cost more to clean—that's straightforward. A 2,000-square-foot office requires different labor than a 10,000-square-foot warehouse. Most commercial cleaners price by square footage, frequency, or a combination of both.

Cleaning Frequency

The more often you clean, the lower the per-visit cost tends to be. Daily janitorial is cheaper per visit than weekly, which is cheaper per visit than monthly. That's because regular maintenance is easier and faster than letting dirt and buildup accumulate.

Service Type

Basic janitorial (vacuuming, emptying trash, wiping surfaces) costs less than deep cleaning. Post-construction cleanup, move-in/move-out cleaning, and specialized services (medical office sanitation, high-touch disinfection) command higher rates because they require more labor and expertise.

Industry & Special Requirements

Medical and dental offices have higher cleaning standards and require specific protocols—that costs more. Retail spaces with customer areas need different attention than office corridors. Industrial facilities may need chemical-safe or heavy-duty procedures. These variations affect pricing.

Facility Condition

A well-maintained space is faster to clean than one that's been neglected. If you've gone months without professional cleaning, the first visit will cost more than the recurring visits that follow.

The Real Cost of Choosing Wrong

The cheapest quote isn't always the best value. Cutting corners on cleaning leads to buildup, pest issues, health risks, and unhappy employees or clients. At T-Town Pristine Clean, we charge fairly for thorough, reliable work that protects your facility and reputation.

Typical Cost Ranges in Tulsa

While we customize every quote, here's a rough idea of what commercial cleaning costs in Tulsa:

  • Small office (500–1,500 sq ft): $200–$400 per weekly cleaning
  • Medium office (2,000–5,000 sq ft): $400–$800 per weekly cleaning
  • Large facility (5,000+ sq ft): $800–$2,000+ per weekly cleaning
  • Deep cleaning: $1,000–$3,000+ depending on size and condition
  • Post-construction: Varies widely; typically quoted per project

These are estimates. Your actual cost depends on your specific needs, and we'll always give you a detailed, custom quote after walking your facility.

How to Get the Best Value

  • Be clear about your needs: Tell us exactly what you want cleaned and how often. This helps us give you an accurate quote.
  • Ask about bundling: Need both regular janitorial and monthly deep cleans? Bundling often comes with discounts.
  • Consider frequency: Switching from monthly to bi-weekly or weekly often lowers your per-visit cost and improves your facility's appearance.
  • Get multiple quotes: Compare apples to apples. Ask each company what's included in their quote and whether they're licensed and insured.
