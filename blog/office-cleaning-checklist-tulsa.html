Office Cleaning Checklist for Tulsa Businesses

A clean office is a productive office. When your workspace is well-maintained, employees stay healthier, clients feel more confident in your professionalism, and your team morale improves. But keeping up with office cleaning can feel overwhelming.

That's why we've created a daily, weekly, and monthly office cleaning checklist for Tulsa businesses. Use this as a reference for what should be cleaned when—or let us handle it for you.

Daily Office Cleaning Tasks

These are tasks that should happen every day, ideally at the end of the workday or early morning before staff arrives:

  • Empty all trash cans and replace liners
  • Sweep and vacuum common areas (hallways, lobby, break rooms)
  • Wipe down desks, tables, and work surfaces
  • Clean and sanitize restrooms (toilets, sinks, mirrors, floors)
  • Mop hard floors in high-traffic areas
  • Wipe door handles, light switches, and other high-touch surfaces
  • Restock paper products (toilet paper, paper towels, soap)
  • Take out recycling

Weekly Office Cleaning Tasks

In addition to daily tasks, set aside time once a week for these deeper-cleaning items:

  • Dust all surfaces, shelves, and baseboards
  • Clean windows and glass doors inside
  • Sanitize break room appliances (microwave, refrigerator handles, coffee maker)
  • Deep clean restrooms (grout, tile, corners)
  • Vacuum carpeted areas thoroughly, including under furniture
  • Wipe down kitchen cabinets and countertops
  • Clean trash can exteriors
  • Sanitize phones, keyboards, and shared equipment

Monthly Office Cleaning Tasks

Once a month, tackle these more intensive cleaning projects:

  • Clean inside windows and window sills
  • Wash walls and remove scuffs and marks
  • Deep clean carpets (consider professional carpet cleaning quarterly)
  • Clean inside cabinets and drawers in common areas
  • Shampoo or deep clean upholstered furniture
  • Clean air vents and replace filters
  • Descale and deep clean restroom fixtures
  • Power wash or spot-clean entry areas

Don't Forget the Details

The little things matter: baseboards, light fixtures, air vents, and corners are often overlooked but visible to clients and staff. A truly clean office catches those details.

Common Office Cleaning Challenges

Managing Multiple Spaces

Larger offices with multiple floors, break rooms, conference rooms, and restrooms need coordinated cleaning. It's easy for certain areas to be neglected.

Employee Health & Sanitation

Shared surfaces—keyboards, door handles, break room appliances—are germ hotspots. Regular, thorough sanitizing is essential, especially post-pandemic.

After-Hours Cleaning

Most office cleaning needs to happen outside business hours so it doesn't disrupt work. Managing this internally is challenging and often requires hiring staff.
